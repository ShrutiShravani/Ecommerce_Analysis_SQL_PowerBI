# E-commerce Analytics Copilot

An end-to-end e-commerce analytics project using **Google BigQuery, SQL, and Power BI** to analyze product performance, customer journeys, acquisition sources, purchase revenue, and device-level behavior.

The project uses the Google Analytics 4 (GA4) public e-commerce demo dataset to explore customer interactions from product views to purchases, identify performance patterns, and support data-driven business decisions.

**Key areas of analysis**
- **Product Performance:** Identify top-viewed and top-purchased products and compare product and category revenue.
- **Customer Journey:** Analyze user activity and engagement across key shopping events, including product views, cart additions, checkout, and purchases.
- **Acquisition Analysis:** Compare purchasing users and recorded purchase revenue across acquisition sources.
- **Revenue Trends:** Examine daily purchase revenue and compare performance across device categories.
- **Data Quality:** Run SQL checks to identify potential data-quality issues and improve confidence in the analysis.

**Tools:** Google BigQuery, SQL, Power BI

**Deliverables:** SQL analysis queries, data-quality checks, and an interactive Power BI dashboard with three report pages.

**Objective:** Turn raw e-commerce event data into clear business insights that help teams understand customer behavior, product performance, and revenue trends.
